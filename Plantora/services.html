02 — Plant care guides

Small care.
Big difference.

Healthy plants start with a few simple habits. Learn the basics and build a routine that works for your space.

01 — Watering

Water with purpose.

Check the soil before watering. Most indoor plants prefer the soil to dry slightly between waterings rather than staying constantly wet.

Find the right light

Place your plant where its natural light needs can be met.

Repot when needed

Give roots more room when growth becomes crowded.

Keep leaves clean

Gently clean dusty leaves so your plant can look and grow its best.

03 — Grow with confidence

Learn.
Care.

Plant care becomes easier when you understand what your plant is telling you. Watch the leaves, feel the soil and adjust with the seasons.
